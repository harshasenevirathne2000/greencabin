Our Story Since 1884

Over 140 Years of Authentic Ceylon Culinary Excellence

Green Cabin, managed under the proud stewardship of Cyril Rodrigo Restaurants, traces its illustrious heritage back to 1884 when it first began as the Ceylon Tea Kiosk at the Colombo Harbour. Over generations, Green Cabin has evolved into a household name, cherished for its legendary puff pastry meat pies, short eats, festive ribbon cakes, and authentic Sri Lankan flavours.

From historic banquet catering to daily morning savouries, our master bakers and chefs preserve the secret family spice blends and traditional recipes that have delighted generations of Sri Lankans and international visitors alike.
